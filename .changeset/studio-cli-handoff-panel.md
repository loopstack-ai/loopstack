Add a `cliHandoff` sidebar feature. When an app registers the `cliHandoff` feature, the workbench shows a "CLI handoff" panel listing prepared `loopstack …` commands as copy-to-clipboard cards, substituting `{workspaceId}` and `{workflowId}` for the run in view (cards stay disabled until their placeholders can be filled).
