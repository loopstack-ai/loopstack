The dashboard can show active workspaces only.

- A "Show active workflows only" toggle beside the card/list switch hides every workspace with no running, waiting or
  pending run. The choice is saved per browser, like the density.
- The summary line still counts all workspaces, and reordering a card with the filter on keeps the hidden workspaces
  in their saved places.
