As the year comes to an end, we want to take a moment to say **thank you**.

2025 was a foundational year for Loopstack. A year of bold ideas, first releases, real-world feedback, and countless conversations with developers who share our belief: AI automation works best when humans stay in control.

This post is a short recap of what we’ve built together so far.

## Presenting Loopstack: Built by Developers, for Developers

Loopstack was created as a developer-first AI automation framework, not a drag-and-drop tool that turns into unmaintainable shadow IT.

From the start, we focused on:

- Code-first workflows
- Version control, testing, and reviews
- Clear APIs and strong abstractions
- Enterprise-grade reliability

Mission-critical automations deserve the same engineering rigor as your core product and Loopstack is built exactly for that.

## Our First Beta Launch & Community Event

One of the most exciting moments of the year was our first beta launch.

We invited developers and AI enthusiasts to our office for a hands-on workshop and mini hackathon. Seeing people build real workflows with something we had worked on for months behind closed doors was incredibly motivating.

But the most valuable part came afterward: honest feedback.

Everyone who joined, tested, and challenged us directly shaped what Loopstack is today.

## Loopstack 0.14: Flexibility & Better Interfaces

With Loopstack 0.14, we took a big step forward in usability and flexibility.

Key highlights:

- Executing workflows with arguments
- Configurable UI forms and actions
- Support for Vercel AI Elements
- Stability and quality improvements across the platform

This release made it easier to adapt workflows to real-world contexts without changing core logic and laid the groundwork for more interactive, user-driven automations.

## Loopstack 0.15: A Major Architectural Leap

Version 0.15 marked our biggest technical milestone so far.

We fundamentally reworked Loopstack’s architecture to make it feel **fully native to NestJS** and dramatically improve developer experience.

Highlights include:

- Native NestJS dependency injection (no custom factories)
- New dependency decorators for workflows, tools, and documents
- Zod-based argument definitions with `@WithArguments`
- Stateless workflows with explicit state via `@WithState`
- Helper methods callable from templates
- A leaner core and first-class testing utilities

This release simplified mental models, improved performance, and made Loopstack easier to reason about, test, and scale.
