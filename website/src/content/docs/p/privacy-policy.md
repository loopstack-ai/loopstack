## 2. Data Processing on This Website

### a) Website Access & Analytics

We use [**Plausible Analytics**](https://plausible.io/), a privacy-friendly analytics service.

- Plausible does **not use cookies** and does not track you across websites.
- All data is aggregated and anonymized (e.g. page views, referrers, device type, country).
- We do not store IP addresses or personal identifiers.

The legal basis for this processing is **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interest in website optimization).

### b) Waitlist Registration

If you register for our waitlist, we collect your **name and email address**.

- We use this data to contact you about updates and access to our services.
- The data will not be shared with third parties.
- You can request deletion at any time (see Section 5).

The legal basis is **Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR** (consent).

### c) Session Cookies (Registered Users Only)

When you log in as a registered user, we use a **session cookie** to authenticate your session and keep you logged in while using the application.

- This cookie is technically necessary for the operation of our service.
- Depending on your settings, the cookie may be stored only for the duration of your browser session (and deleted when you close the browser) or as a persistent cookie that allows you to remain logged in across sessions until it expires.
- No tracking or profiling takes place with this cookie.

The legal basis for this processing is **Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR** (performance of a contract, providing the login functionality).

## 4. Data Retention

- Analytics data is anonymized and not personally identifiable.
- Waitlist data is stored until you request deletion or we no longer need it for the stated purpose.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the following rights under the GDPR:

- Access to your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR)
- Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
- Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
- Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR)
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)
