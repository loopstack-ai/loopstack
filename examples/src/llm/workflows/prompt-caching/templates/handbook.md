# Riverside Bikes rider handbook

## 1. Accounts

A rider needs an account to unlock a bike. An account is created in the Riverside Bikes app with an email address, a mobile phone number and a payment card. Riders must be at least 16 years old. Riders between 16 and 18 need the consent of a parent or guardian, which the app collects during sign-up.

Each account can rent up to two bikes at the same time. The second bike is meant for a companion; the account holder is responsible for both bikes and for all fees on them.

An account can be paused for up to six months, for example during a long trip. A paused account pays no membership fee and cannot unlock bikes. Riders can close an account at any time in the app under Settings, Account, Close account. Open fees are charged before the account closes.

## 2. Plans and prices

Riverside Bikes offers three plans.

- **Pay as you go.** No monthly fee. Unlocking a bike costs 1.00 EUR, and every started minute costs 0.20 EUR.
- **Monthly.** 12.00 EUR per month. The first 45 minutes of every ride are free. After that, every started minute costs 0.10 EUR. Unlocking is free.
- **Annual.** 99.00 EUR per year, paid at once. The same ride rules as the Monthly plan apply, and annual members get two free day passes per year for guests.

E-bikes cost an extra 0.10 EUR per minute on every plan, from the first minute. The free 45 minutes of the Monthly and Annual plans do not cover the e-bike surcharge.

A **day pass** costs 9.00 EUR and gives 24 hours of rides of up to 60 minutes each, starting when the pass is bought. A ride longer than 60 minutes on a day pass costs 0.20 EUR for every started minute beyond the 60.

Students with a valid student card get 30 percent off the Monthly and Annual plans. The discount is applied after the student card is verified in the app, which usually takes one working day.

Prices include VAT. Riverside Bikes sends an invoice for every month in which the account had any charge.

## 3. Unlocking and returning a bike

To unlock a bike, scan the QR code on the handlebar with the app, or type the five-digit bike number. The lock opens within a few seconds and the ride starts.

Bikes must be returned to a Riverside Bikes station. Push the bike into a free dock until the light on the dock turns green. The ride ends when the light is green and the app shows the ride summary. If all docks at a station are full, the app shows the nearest station with free docks and adds 15 free minutes to the ride, so that the rider is not charged for the extra way.

A bike that is left outside a station is charged a fee of 25.00 EUR, plus the minutes until the bike is returned to a station by the service team.

Riders can pause a ride for up to 30 minutes, for example to buy something in a shop. During a pause the bike is locked with its built-in cable lock, and the minute price continues to apply.

## 4. E-bikes and batteries

E-bikes have a range of about 50 kilometres with a full battery. The app shows the battery level of every e-bike before it is unlocked. E-bikes with less than 20 percent battery cannot be unlocked.

Batteries are charged at the station. Riders never change or charge batteries themselves. If the battery runs out during a ride, the e-bike can still be ridden like a normal bike, without motor support.

The motor supports riders up to a speed of 25 kilometres per hour.

## 5. Damage, faults and accidents

Before a ride, riders should check the brakes, the tyres, the light and the bell. A bike with a fault can be reported in the app before the ride starts; the ride is then cancelled at no cost and the bike is blocked until the service team repairs it.

If a fault appears during a ride, return the bike to the nearest station and report it in the app. Riverside Bikes refunds the ride if the fault is confirmed.

Riders are not charged for normal wear. A rider who damages a bike on purpose or through gross negligence pays for the repair, up to a maximum of 300.00 EUR.

In case of an accident, make sure everyone is safe first and call the emergency number 112 if anyone is injured. Then report the accident in the app under Help, Report an accident. The service team calls back within one hour.

A stolen bike must be reported to the police and to Riverside Bikes within 24 hours. A rider who reports a theft in time and sends the police report pays no fee for the stolen bike. A rider who does not report the theft pays 600.00 EUR.

## 6. Payments and refunds

Riverside Bikes charges the payment card on the account. Ride fees are charged when the ride ends. Plan fees are charged at the start of each plan period.

If a card payment fails, the account is blocked until the open amount is paid. Riders can pay the open amount in the app with another card.

Refunds go back to the card that was charged, and usually arrive within five working days. Refund requests must be sent within 30 days of the ride, in the app under Help, My rides.

Riverside Bikes does not refund the remaining months of a Monthly plan or the remaining part of an Annual plan when an account is closed, except when the rider moves out of the service area and shows proof of the new address.

## 7. Service area and opening hours

The service area covers the city centre, the university campus and the riverside districts. The map in the app shows the exact border. Riding outside the service area is allowed, but the bike must be returned to a station inside it.

Stations are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. During storm warnings, Riverside Bikes can close the service for safety. The app shows a message when the service is closed, and rides that are already running can be finished.

## 8. Safety rules

Riders must follow the traffic rules. Wearing a helmet is recommended but not required. Only one person may ride a bike; children may not be carried, and the front basket holds up to 10 kilograms.

Riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs is not allowed and leads to the closure of the account.

## 9. Privacy

Riverside Bikes stores the start and end station, the time and the route of every ride, to calculate prices and to find bikes. Route data is deleted after 90 days. Riders can download all data stored about them in the app under Settings, Privacy, Download my data.

## 10. Contact

The support team answers in the app chat every day from 7:00 to 22:00. Outside these hours, the app chat answers questions from this handbook, and the service team handles accidents and thefts at any time.
